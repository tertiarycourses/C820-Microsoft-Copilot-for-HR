# Asteron Services - HR Operations Coordinator Role Profile

**Source ID:** ROLE-01  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** People Operations Manager  
**Status:** Approved synthetic training source  
**Effective date:** 1 July 2026

## Role outcome

Coordinate employee-service cases and onboarding activities accurately, maintain clear operating records and communicate next actions to employees and internal service owners.

## Core responsibilities

1. Receive, classify and track employee-service and onboarding cases.
2. Maintain weekly spreadsheet reports and verify counts before circulation.
3. Coordinate with People Operations, hiring managers, IT and Learning Operations.

## Required job-related criteria

### C1 - Service case handling

Evidence should show work receiving, resolving, routing or tracking service cases, requests or operational issues.

### C2 - Spreadsheet reporting

Evidence should show spreadsheet use for recurring reporting, reconciliation, validation or operational tracking.

### C3 - Stakeholder communication

Evidence should show structured communication or coordination across teams, service owners, customers or employees.

## Excluded criteria

Do not use or infer age, race, religion, sex, marital status, family responsibilities, disability, health, nationality, photographs, names, residential location, personality, salary history or any information not justified by the role requirements.

## Decision boundary

The evidence assistant may cite resume evidence, identify unknowns and propose rubric scores. A trained hiring panel owns all next-step decisions and structured interview questions.
